Storm's Sailing

Thirteen years of love — for Amie, always

A golden goodbye

Storm was a bright-hearted girl for all of her thirteen years — white fur catching the last light, spirit like weather: bold, changing, unforgettable. This page is a meadow at sunset for Amie, who walked beside her through every season and still lifts a hand to say goodbye.

Storm was never an easy dog. She asked for patience and truth. She was a teaching dog: the lessons were hard, and they were worth keeping. Now she runs the path ahead, toward a warm horizon — not gone from love, only sailing a little farther than we can follow.

When the daisies sway and the sky turns gold, may you feel her still — in the quiet wave, the familiar step, the love that stays after the light softens.

“Storm has not been an easy dog. But she has been a teaching dog. Learning from her has been tough, but appreciated.” — Amie
