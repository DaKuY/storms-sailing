Remembering Storm

Storm was a remarkable girl — fierce, tender, and unmistakably herself for all of her thirteen years. As she sails on beyond our reach, this little harbor holds a place for the stories that belong to her and to the person who loved her through every season: Amie.

Storm was never a simple dog. She asked for patience, honesty, and courage. In return she offered lessons that do not fade: how to show up on hard days, how forgiveness can live beside frustration, and how love can be complicated and still be true.

May every wave on this page carry a moment you still feel in your hands — a wet nose, a stubborn walk, a quiet evening when she chose to stay close. She was here. She mattered. She still does.

“Storm has not been an easy dog. But she has been a teaching dog. Learning from her has been tough, but appreciated.” — Amie
